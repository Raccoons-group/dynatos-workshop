# Issue #2: Show allergens on the menu

1. **Asked:** show allergens on Forkful's menu, so customers know what's in a dish before they order. Two reasons: customers keep phoning restaurants to ask, and someone with a nut allergy ordered a curry that contained almonds.
2. **Already decided:** only that allergens appear on the menu. Forkful stores no allergen data today, and restaurants have no screen for editing a dish.
3. **Open:** which allergen list to use, and whether to show "may contain traces".
4. **Open:** who enters the data, given restaurants "won't like another form".
5. **Open:** whether customers can filter dishes out, and what to show when a restaurant hasn't given any allergens.
