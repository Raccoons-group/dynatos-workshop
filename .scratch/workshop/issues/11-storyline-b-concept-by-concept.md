## Question

Outline the deck concept by concept, for example context → skills → specs → harness → verification → team adoption. Keep the same meta opener and the same skills content as Storyline A.

Give each slide a title and a one-line intent, grouped by agenda block, with time estimates. Use the same format as Storyline A so the two can be compared directly.

## Answer

Outline: [assets/storyline-b.md](../assets/storyline-b.md). Reviewed with Matthias on 20 and 22 Oct 2026. He agreed it is a fair representation of Storyline B. Choosing between A and B is left to [Choose the storyline](12-choose-the-storyline.md).

- **The spine is six layers of the harness around the agent, one concept each:** context, skills, specs, checks, verification and review, and team.
  - Each layer is a guide that steers the agent, a sensor that catches it, or the team that keeps the harness alive (Böckeler's harness).
  - Each layer gets one idea, at most one short live demo, and one exhibit from this repo.
- **The opener is general (Matthias's correction to the first draft).**
  - The talk opens on AI-assisted development in general: code is no longer the bottleneck, AI is an amplifier, the agent is a new hire who forgets everything.
  - The skills deck's own opening (p2–3, "the real gap is expertise, not intelligence" and the failure modes) now opens the skills chapter.
  - The meta opener shows the map, a research note and the PRs in plain words. The wayfinder pages (p18–21) moved to the specs chapter.
- **One frame from morning to Day 2.**
  - Six layer cards appear empty in the opener and fill from each group's PR during the afternoon.
  - The retrospective follows ticket 14's outline, with its middle sections recut as the layers. "Where the agent went wrong" places each mistake in a layer.
  - The skill lab asks which layer a fix belongs in. The action plan is a grid: six layers × (quick win, longer step, owner).
- **What's the same as A:** the fixed gates and the groups' free choices (from tickets 08, 13, 14 and 16), the setup round, the case kickoff facts, and the interludes.
- **Size.**
  - 100 slides and about 318 minutes over the seven blocks.
  - The talk is exactly 90 minutes, with five independent live demos (21 minutes in total). The demos run on this repo or on a Forkful sibling story, never on the groups' epic.
  - Slides 54–73 match the B column in the [reporter sketch](../assets/reporter-and-retrospective.md).
- **Weaknesses to weigh in ticket 12.**
  - No end-to-end story before the hands-on: the order of steps arrives only at the kickoff.
  - The skills deck is split across chapters, though p2–6 and p10 stay together.
  - 90 minutes of theory before the case, with verification (the strongest lever) arriving around minute 65.
- **Not done:** the prototype skill's throwaway-branch commit. This session was told to commit nothing.
